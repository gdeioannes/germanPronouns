1The imperative tells someone what to do: Komm! Wartet! Setzen Sie sich! German has three, because there are three ways to say you: du, ihr and Sie.2Each one comes from the present tense (Chart 25). The verb goes first, and a split-off prefix goes to the end: Ruf mich an! Below: twelve verbs, then how to make it sound friendly.

Look here first: three ways to say do it!

The one idea: start from the present

du nimmst → Nimm!

Take the du form, drop du and -st. What is left is the command, and e → i stays: Nimm!
