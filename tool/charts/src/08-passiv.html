The one idea

The Passiv turns the sentence around. What happens matters, who does it does not. The yellow Akkusativ moves to the front and becomes the subject.

Use it when

Nobody knows who: Mein Rad wurde gestohlen.

Everybody knows who: Der Müll wird montags abgeholt.

Rules, recipes, science: Die Eier werden zehn Minuten gekocht.

One pattern, every tensewerden carries the tense, the Partizip II never changes
