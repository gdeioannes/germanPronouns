1Each person has three forms. The job in the sentence decides: the one who does it (Nominativ), the one it is done to (Akkusativ), the one who gets or is helped (Dativ).2Read down a column: the sentence stays the same, only the pronoun changes. Where one box covers two columns, the word is the same in both cases.

order
two pronouns: Akkusativ firstIch gebe es ihm. · Ich zeige sie dir. · with a noun, the pronoun first: Ich gebe ihm das Buch.
du or Sie?du: friends, family, children · Sie: strangers, at work: Kannst du mir helfen? Können Sie mir helfen?
watch out
