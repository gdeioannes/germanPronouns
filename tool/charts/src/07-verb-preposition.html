1Look here first: the verb picks its preposition. English does not help: wait for = warten auf.2Read the map like a metro: a line is a preposition, a stop is a verb, the colour is the case.

The one idea

Learn the verb and its preposition as one word: warten auf, never just warten. The colour of the line gives you the case for free.
