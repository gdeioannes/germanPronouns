  1. Scan the chart, land on the chartThe code is permanent, like the codes in the books. It opens this page, not a generic quiz list.
  2. Tap any cellYou hear the form, see it in two sentences, and get the reason for the case in one line.
  3. Practise only that cellA short run on exactly the form you tapped. A whole row or a whole case works the same way.
  4. The chart becomes your mapEvery answer, here or in any other quiz, colours the cell it belongs to. Weak cells are where to practise next.
  5. Print your own copyThe same sheet as in the book, with your weak cells marked.

3 · From any quizA mistake points back to the chart

When a learner misses a question anywhere in the course, the explanation shows where that form sits on the chart. The chart becomes the mental map, and the book is advertised at the moment it is useful.
