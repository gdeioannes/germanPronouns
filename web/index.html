Learn German, Spanish, Chinese and English with free interactive quizzes

Language Quiz is a free language-learning app with quick, focused grammar and vocabulary quizzes. Every sentence has audio, speaking exercises give instant feedback, and courses follow the CEFR levels from A1 to C2 — pass one sub-level to unlock the next. No account, no sign-up: it runs right here in your browser on phone, tablet or desktop.

Made for the way you learn

Working toward a certificate?

Follow a step-by-step CEFR path from A1 to C2 — pass each sub-level to unlock the next, with reading, listening and speaking checks.

Untangling grammar?

Articles, pronouns, cases and conjugations explained first, then drilled in short quizzes until they stick.

Collecting words?

Themed vocabulary with audio on every sentence, a word library, and printable worksheets for offline practice.

Just curious?

Taste a new language in an afternoon — first words of Czech, Chinese characters on a drawing canvas, everyday Spanish.
