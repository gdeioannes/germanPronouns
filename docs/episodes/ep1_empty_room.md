# Episode 1 — "The Empty Room" (de_cert_a1, first half of A1.1)

Episode-specific material for the first Maya mystery. The general rules it
builds on (art style, Maya, language mixing, voice casting, character
creation) live in `docs/story_bible.md`; the module JSON schema in
`docs/story_module_schema.md`.

## Premise

Maya's flatmate **Jonas** vanished overnight: his WG room is stripped bare,
with a torn note on the floor. With the learner as her remote assistant,
Maya follows three leads (a locked phone, a filled-in form, a scrambled
group chat) to find Lena — and learns Jonas moved out in secret.
Cliffhanger: why? (→ Episode 2, second half of A1.1).

Covers: numbers 0–20, personal pronouns, articles/plural, sein & haben,
W-Fragen, introductions, regular verbs (quests `quest_a1_1_zahlen` …
`quest_a1_1_diktat_steckbrief`).

## Structure (agreed in planning)

Intro → Ch.1 The Note (reading/cloze) → Ch.2 The Voicemail (listening) →
HUB, any order: Ch.3 The Phone Call (dial orderedPick → listen → build
replies), Ch.4 The Steckbrief (bigText cloze), Ch.5 The Chat Log
(orderedPick sort) → Ch.6 Confrontation (dialogue) → resolution +
cliffhanger. Credibility 3; pools randomize PIN, names, cities, room
numbers; ≥8 min play floor; micro-texts from Maya between chapters.

Lighting per the bible's vocabulary: door shaft for investigation, lamp
cone for Ch.6, dusk window for calm beats, torch noir at most 2–3 times.
