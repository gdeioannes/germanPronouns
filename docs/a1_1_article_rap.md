# DER DIE DAS – Kim's Day (the A1.1 Article Story Rap)

All 84 nouns with an article from the A1.1 vocabulary deck (`quest_a1_1_wortschatz`), rapped as one day
in Berlin. The story is told in English, and every German noun arrives WITH its article inside a line
whose context explains it. No translation lines: the meaning comes from the story.
~90 bpm boom-bap. Stress the article every time. Colour code: DER blue, DIE red, DAS green.

---

## HOOK (x2)
DER, DIE, DAS – that's the beat, that's the bass,
DER, DIE, DAS – put the article in place.
Blue is DER, red is DIE, green is DAS,
say it with the noun and the word will last.

## VERSE 1 – Waking up
DIE Uhr on the wall is ticking, it's seven,
DIE Sonne comes up and DER Himmel's blue heaven.
DAS Fenster is open, I feel the cold air,
DAS Bett is so warm, but I can't stay there.
DIE Lampe still on from reading last night,
DAS Buch on the floor, DIE Seite in sight.
I grab DER Schlüssel, I grab DIE Tasche,
pat DER Hund on the head – he's a good little Lasche.
DIE Katze says nothing, she just looks mean,
I open DIE Tür and step on the scene.

## VERSE 2 – Breakfast with the family
In DIE Küche DER Tisch is set for ten,
DER Stuhl is wobbly, I fix it again.
DER Vater makes coffee, DIE Mutter cuts DAS Brot,
DER Apfel for me, DIE Banane – oh no, it's gone, it's not!
DER Hunger is big and DER Durst is real,
DER Bruder, DIE Schwester fight over the meal.
DER Ball rolls in, DER Junge did that,
DAS Mädchen laughs loud, DAS Kind cries „Wo ist DIE Katz'?"
DER Opa, DIE Oma are sitting right there,
DER Großvater jokes and DIE Großmutter stares.
DIE Eltern say „Ruhe!", DIE Geschwister go on,
DER Sohn gets a hug, DIE Tochter gets one.
DAS Jahr is still young, but DIE Zeit runs away,
grab DER Schlüssel, we're going – it's a big day.

## VERSE 3 – Into the city

DIE Stadt has DIE Million people, that's a fact.

## Memory tricks the story uses
- **Men and -er jobs = DER**: Lehrer, Kellner, Verkäufer, Schüler, Amerikaner, Einwohner, Kilometer, Computer.
- **Women and most -e words = DIE**: Lampe, Katze, Banane, Tasche, Küche, Schule, Miete, Seite, Sonne. Exceptions sit in DER lines: Junge, Kollege, Buchstabe, Vorname, Nachname.
- **-chen is always DAS**: Mädchen, even though it is a girl.
- **Plural-only = DIE**: Eltern, Geschwister.
- **Countries normally have no article**, but DIE Schweiz, DIE Türkei, DIE USA keep DIE.
