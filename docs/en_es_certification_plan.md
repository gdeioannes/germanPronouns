# en_es — Certification Spanish A1–C2 (English → Spanish)

Course for English speakers learning Spanish. `speak 🇬🇧 → learn 🇪🇸`,
`uiLang: UiLang.en`, `learnLocale: 'es-ES'`. All teaching text is **English**;
every answer/passage is **Spanish**.

**Anchoring:** CEFR only (A1–C2 descriptors and public can-do statements). No
exam brand names (see `docs/ip_copyright_review.md`). The Spanish taught is
**pan-Hispanic neutral**: `ustedes` is the default "you all", `vosotros` is
introduced and flagged as Spain-only; vocabulary notes major regional variants
(coche/carro, ordenador/computadora) without preferring one.

## Contrastive spine E1–E30 (English ↔ Spanish)

Tagged ⚑E<n> in Help Memory tips.

**Sound & spelling (E1–E6):** E1 five pure vowels, never reduced (no schwa);
E2 r/rr tap & trill; E3 ñ, ll/y, j (/x/), silent h; E4 c/z/qu/g spelling rules;
E5 written accents mark stress (and meaning: si/sí, el/él); E6 question &
exclamation marks open inverted (¿ ¡), intonation questions.

**Grammar (E7–E24):** E7 every noun has gender, articles & adjectives agree
(el/la, -o/-a, plural -s/-es); E8 adjectives usually follow the noun; E9 verbs
conjugate for person — subject pronouns drop (E10 pro-drop: "hablo" = I speak);
E11 ser vs estar; E12 gustar-type verbs (me gusta + backwards subject);
E13 reflexives (levantarse) & daily routine; E14 preterite vs imperfect —
the two pasts English merges; E15 por vs para; E16 personal a; E17 object
pronouns lo/la/le & clitic placement (before the verb, attached to infinitive);
E18 the subjunctive — present (quiero que vengas), E19 past subjunctive &
si-clauses (si tuviera); E20 commands (affirmative/negative, usted forms);
E21 future & conditional tenses (one word, not "will"); E22 comparisons
(más…que, tan…como) & absolute -ísimo; E23 se-constructions (passive/impersonal:
se habla español); E24 relative clauses & que/quien/lo que.

**Lexicon & register (E25–E30):** E25 false friends (embarazada, actualmente,
realizar, asistir, carpeta, éxito…); E26 articles where English drops them
(la vida, los lunes, el 90 %); E27 two verbs "to know" (saber/conocer) and
other splits (pedir/preguntar, llevar/traer); E28 vosotros vs ustedes & voseo
awareness (E-variant notes); E29 word order flexibility & topicalisation;
E30 register — formal usted, written connectors, subjunctive-heavy courtesy
for the C-levels.

## Modules (12, each gated, chain order)

Same shape as `es_en`: ~23 quizzes per module — 8 fill · 3 vocab · 3 speak ·
2 reading · 2 listening · 2 dictation · 2 speaking (mid checkpoint + closing) ·
1 big text. speakRepeat carries E1–E6; dictation targets accents, gender
endings and clitics; fills carry the grammar spine.

- **A1.1 · First sounds & selves** — pronunciation (E1–E3), ser + pronouns, gender & articles (E7), numbers, greetings, nationality, hay.
- **A1.2 · Daily life** — -ar/-er/-ir present (E9–E10), estar & location, ser/estar first pass (E11), gustar (E12), reflexive routine (E13), family, time, days (E26).
- **A2.1 · The past, twice** — preterite regular + key irregulars, imperfect, the contrast (E14), childhood & anecdotes, hace…que.
- **A2.2 · Plans, wants & city life** — ir a + infinitive, tener que/hay que, object pronouns (E17), personal a (E16), comparisons (E22), directions, food & shopping.
- **B1.1 · Opinions & feelings** — present subjunctive after emotion/wish/doubt (E18), commands (E20), por/para (E15), saber/conocer (E27), health & advice.
- **B1.2 · Stories & conditions** — preterite vs imperfect mastery, past perfect, si + present/future, se-constructions (E23), narration connectors.
- **B2.1 · Hypothesis & argument** — past subjunctive + si tuviera…daría (E19), conditional, modal nuance (deber/deber de), relative clauses (E24), debate language.
- **B2.2 · Nuance & reporting** — reported speech & tense shifts, subjunctive vs indicative triggers, future/conditional of probability (E21), false friends (E25), regional variation (E28).
- **C1.1 · Abstraction & precision** — advanced subjunctive (concessives, aunque + mood), nominal style, collocations, connectors of cause/consequence, lo + adjective.
- **C1.2 · Register & rhetoric** — formal usted register (E30), written-Spanish connectors, cleft/topicalised orders (E29), courtesy conditional, academic verbs.
- **C2.1 · Style & idiom** — idioms & proverbs, humour & irony, subtle mood contrasts (indicative vs subjunctive meaning shifts), diminutives/augmentatives.
- **C2.2 · Mastery** — synthesis: style editing, dialect-aware comprehension, rhetorical structure, the hardest mood/aspect traps, near-native polish.
