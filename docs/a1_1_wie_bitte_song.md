# WIE BITTE? – Max's first date (the A1.1 "help me understand" rap)

Max from "Ich bin Max" has a date with Lena from Berlin. His German is ten words, and five of them are
"Wie bitte?". One evening, one story: a loud bar, a library, a restaurant, the bill. Every help
phrase is the natural thing to say at that moment, and what happens next shows what it means.
Where the story can't show the meaning, a few English words say it right after the German.
The joke comes from the phrase working too well: "too loud" sends them to a library, "slowly"
turns Lena into a robot, and "Was bedeutet Rechnung?" is the most expensive question of the night.

## Notes

- One story, in order: every scene follows from the one before (too loud → quiet place → library
  closes → dinner → the bill → a second date).
- The meaning of most phrases comes from what happens next: "zu laut" → they leave; "Langsam" → she
  goes slow; "Können Sie das erklären?" → the waiter mimes a snail. A short English echo follows only
  where the story can't show it.
- The outro turns "Noch einmal?" and "Langsam" into a sweet ending: the same words, a new meaning.
- "Können Sie …" is the polite Sie form for the waiter; Lena is du.
- No exam names and no level talk in the lyrics.
